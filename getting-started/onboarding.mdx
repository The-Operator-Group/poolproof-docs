## How Onboarding Works

Most software vendors ask you to commit first and figure out the rest later. PoolProof inverts that model. Your data is migrated and your account is configured before you sign anything — so you make your decision with real information, not a sales demo.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Book a Discovery Call">
    Reach out to the PoolProof team by phone at **(210) 245-7495** or by email at **info@poolproof.com**. On the call, your dedicated onboarding specialist takes the time to understand your business: how many routes you run, which software you're coming from, how your billing is structured, and what your team looks like. This isn't a sales pitch — it's the intake session that shapes everything that follows.
  </Step>

  <Step title="PoolProof Migrates Your Data — Before You Sign">
    Your onboarding specialist pulls your data out of your current system and loads it directly into PoolProof. You don't fill out templates or spend a weekend reformatting spreadsheets. The migration covers:

    - **Customer records** — names, service addresses, billing addresses, contact details, access codes (gate codes, lockbox combinations, entry notes)
    - **Routes and service schedules** — stop assignments, service days, route structure
    - **Service history** — visit dates, chemical readings, technician notes, tasks completed, chemicals and quantities used
    - **Billing data** — open invoices, payment history, service plan pricing per customer

    <Tip>
      You log in and see your own business — your customers, your routes, your history — already inside PoolProof before you've committed to anything. If it's not the right fit, you walk away and owe nothing. The migration work is on us.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review Your Account">
    Once migration is complete, your onboarding specialist walks you through the populated account. Spot-check your customers, confirm route structure, verify pricing, and review service history records. This is where you make your decision with real data in front of you rather than a promise.

    Common things to verify during your review:
    - Customer count and address accuracy
    - Service plan assignments and pricing per stop
    - Route assignments and weekly schedule
    - Equipment records and access information
    - Open invoices and billing balances
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure PoolProof for Your Workflow">
    With your data confirmed, your onboarding specialist configures PoolProof around the way your operation actually runs. This includes:

    - **Service areas** — defining the geographic territories your business covers and assigning customers and technicians accordingly
    - **Service plans** — setting up your billing tiers, chemical protocols, required readings, process steps, and periodic maintenance schedules (filter cleans, salt cell cleanings, equipment inspections)
    - **Billing settings** — payment terms, automated collections, tax configuration, QuickBooks integration
    - **Notification templates** — service completion emails, customer-facing SMS and email communications
    - **Product catalog** — the chemicals, equipment, and services your team uses in the field and on invoices
    - **User accounts and roles** — configuring access levels for office staff, technicians, and managers

    <Tip>
      If your workflow has a quirk that doesn't fit a standard configuration, tell your onboarding specialist. PoolProof is built to fit your business, and the configuration phase is the right time to surface edge cases.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Train Your Team">
    Before you go live, your onboarding specialist trains your entire team — office staff and field technicians. Training is hands-on and uses your real account, so your team learns against their actual customers, routes, and workflows rather than dummy data.

    Office training covers:
    - Customer and route management
    - Invoicing, payments, and collections
    - Quoting and work order workflows
    - Communication tools and the shared inbox

    Technician training covers:
    - The mobile app and stop workflow
    - Recording chemical readings and completing process steps
    - Capturing photos and completing periodic maintenance tasks
    - Handling skipped stops and exception reporting
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go Live">
    Once your team is trained and your account is fully configured, you flip the switch. Most customers go live in **30 days or less** from their first call — many sooner. Your onboarding specialist stays available through your first service week to handle any questions that come up in the field.

    <Tip>
      Run your first billing cycle with your onboarding specialist available. Automated collections, QuickBooks sync, and payment retries all behave differently than manual billing — a quick check-in after your first run saves time later.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the Migration Covers

White-glove migration is included with every PoolProof subscription — a $5,000 value at no additional cost. The table below shows what the PoolProof team migrates for you.

| Data Category | What's Included |
|---|---|
| **Customers** | Names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, secondary contacts, access codes |
| **Service Schedules** | Plan assignment, frequency, service day, route assignment, per-customer pricing |
| **Service History** | Visit dates, chemical readings, chemicals used, technician notes, tasks completed |
| **Equipment Records** | Pump, filter, heater, salt cell, sanitation type, automation system details |
| **Billing Data** | Open invoices, paid history, credits, service agreement records |
| **Routes** | Stop assignments, route structure, service day mapping |

<Note>
  Saved payment methods (credit cards on autopay) are vaulted with your current payment processor, not your software. Your onboarding specialist will walk you through the payment-method transition specifically — either a compliant vault-to-vault transfer or a customer re-authorization campaign — before your first billing run.
</Note>

## Frequently Asked Questions

  <Accordion title="Can I sign up for PoolProof online without talking to someone?">

    No. PoolProof migrates your data and populates your account before you sign anything or pay a dollar. You log in, see your own business inside PoolProof, and then decide. If it's not the right fit, you walk away and owe nothing.
