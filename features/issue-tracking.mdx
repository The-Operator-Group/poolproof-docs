PoolProof's issue tracking system gives every customer problem a proper home — from the moment it's reported to the moment it's solved. This page explains how issues are created, how they move through their lifecycle, and how they connect to work orders, quotes, and customer records to give you complete visibility over every open problem in your business.

## Issues vs. Work Orders

Before diving in, it helps to understand the distinction:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Issues" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    **Track problems.** An issue is "customer's pump is making a grinding noise" or "pool turned green after the storm." Issues capture what's wrong, where, and how urgent it is.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Work Orders" icon="wrench">
    **Track solutions.** A work order is "replace pump motor at 123 Main St on Tuesday." Work orders schedule the labor that fixes the problem.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Issues and work orders are linked in PoolProof, giving you full traceability from the first complaint to the completed repair.

## How Issues Are Created

Problems reach you through multiple channels. PoolProof captures them all in one organized system.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="AI-Powered Customer Message">
    Customers report problems by text (SMS), WhatsApp, or email — in their own words. When a message comes in, PoolProof's AI analyzes the content to detect the issue type and urgency, then asks the customer targeted follow-up questions to gather the details you need.

    The AI doesn't require perfectly worded reports. When a customer sends something like *"my pool is green and my husband is furious,"* the AI understands the urgency, identifies the problem type, and asks the right follow-ups — things like *"Is the pool still circulating?"* or *"When was the last service visit?"*

    Once the conversation is complete, PoolProof creates a fully structured issue with:
    - An auto-generated summary
    - Issue category assigned automatically
    - Priority level set based on urgency signals in the conversation

    <Note>
      Customers never need to log in or create an account. They simply reply to a message and PoolProof handles the rest.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Technician Observation">
    Techs in the field often spot problems that customers haven't noticed yet — a cracked skimmer basket, a failing O-ring, low water from a slow leak. Techs can create issues directly from the mobile app during a service visit. The issue is automatically linked to the customer and service address they're currently working at.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Manual Staff Entry">
    For problems reported by phone, spotted during a billing review, or escalated by a customer in person, any staff member can create an issue manually from the issue dashboard. Customer and address information populate automatically once you select the account.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Issue Lifecycle

Every issue in PoolProof follows a clear path from open to resolved.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open">
    A problem is identified and logged. The issue has a category, priority, and is linked to a customer and service address. It appears in the open issues queue for your team to act on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="In Progress">
    A work order is created from the issue (one click — details below) and the tech is dispatched. The issue status updates to reflect that a resolution is underway.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resolved">
    When the linked work order is completed, PoolProof automatically closes the associated issue. No manual status update required — the system handles it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Auto-close keeps your issue queue accurate without relying on anyone to remember to update statuses. When the work is done, the issue closes.
</Info>

## Creating a Work Order from an Issue

When an issue is ready to be scheduled, convert it to a work order in one click. PoolProof pre-fills the work order with the customer's name, service address, and a reference back to the originating issue — no re-entering information you already have.

The work order and issue stay linked throughout the process, so anyone looking at either record can see the full picture: what the problem was, what work was scheduled, and whether it's been completed.

## Converting an Issue to a Quote

Not every problem needs a simple service visit — some require new equipment, a repair estimate, or a formal proposal. Convert any issue directly to a quote with one click. The customer and address information carry over automatically, and you can build out the line items from there.

<Tip>
  Use issue-to-quote conversion for equipment failures, renovation requests, or any situation where the customer needs to approve costs before you schedule work.
</Tip>

## Issue Details and Customer Context

When you open any issue in PoolProof, you see the full picture — not just the problem in isolation:

<Accordion title="What's visible on an issue record?">
  - **Customer profile** — name, contact information, preferred communication method
  - **Service address** — the specific property the issue relates to
  - **Service history** — all previous visits for that address, including chemical readings and notes
  - **Other open issues** — any other outstanding problems for the same customer
  - **Linked work orders** — any scheduled or completed work tied to this issue
  - **Category and priority** — problem type and urgency level for triage and reporting
  - **Issue timeline** — when it was created, who created it, and all status changes
</Accordion>

Viewing a customer record shows the same relationship in reverse — all open and resolved issues for that customer appear on their profile, so you always have context before a call or visit.

## Categories and Priorities

PoolProof organizes issues by problem type so you can prioritize effectively, route intelligently, and spot patterns over time. Categorization happens automatically for AI-created issues and is set during manual entry.

Use priorities to sort your queue:

| Priority | When to Use |
|---|---|
| **Urgent** | Pool is unsafe, non-functional, or the customer is escalating |
| **High** | Equipment failure or water quality problem affecting usability |
| **Normal** | Maintenance concerns, non-urgent repairs, customer questions |
| **Low** | Cosmetic issues, future improvements, wish-list items |

<Warning>
  Don't let urgent issues sit in the queue. PoolProof surfaces them at the top of the issue list, but it's up to your team to act on them promptly.
</Warning>
