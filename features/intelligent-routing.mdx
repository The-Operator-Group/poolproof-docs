Intelligent Routing is PoolProof's automated route-building and route-management system. This page explains how the optimization algorithm works, how the Digital Dispatcher keeps your week running when plans change, how to adjust routes manually, and how customer replies to "on the way" texts reach the right technician automatically.

## How Route Optimization Works

PoolProof builds routes from the templates you control, then continuously optimizes them to reduce drive time and balance workloads across your team. Every week starts from a known structure — your ideal week — rather than a blank slate.

When you trigger an optimization, the algorithm works through four stages in sequence:

<Steps>
  ### Group by Service Day
  PoolProof first partitions your customer list by their assigned service day — Monday pools stay with Monday pools, Wednesday pools with Wednesday pools. This keeps your weekly cadence intact.

  ### Cluster Geographically
  Within each day's pool of customers, the algorithm groups nearby addresses together. Tight geographic clusters eliminate the criss-cross driving that adds miles without adding stops.

  ### Balance by Duration
  Clusters are then distributed across your available technicians so that each tech's total service time for the day is as even as possible. One tech doesn't finish at noon while another is still running at 6 PM.

  ### Consolidate Undersized Routes
  If any route ends up too light — too few stops or too little drive time to justify a dedicated day — PoolProof folds those stops into adjacent routes. You end up with fewer, fuller routes instead of several thin ones.
</Steps>

The result is a set of efficient routes that reflect your business today, not six months ago when you last rebuilt them by hand.

<Tip>
  Optimization gives you a strong starting point, but you stay in control. After running the algorithm, drag stops between routes, reorder stops within a route, or move a customer to a different service day. PoolProof recalculates estimated distance and duration as you edit.
</Tip>

## Route Templates

Every week starts from a versioned route template — your ideal week, built once and tuned as things change. Templates give the optimizer a baseline to work from and preserve the structure of your week even as individual stops shift.

<Info>
  Route templates are versioned, which means PoolProof keeps a history of changes. You can see how a route has evolved, compare routing decisions, and roll back if a change doesn't work out.
</Info>

When your customer base shifts — you add a new neighborhood, lose a cluster of accounts, or bring on an extra tech — PoolProof flags what needs attention and helps you adjust without rebuilding from scratch.

## Adjusting Routes Manually

You have full control at every level of the route:

- **Drag stops between routes** — move a customer from one technician's day to another
- **Reorder stops within a route** — change the sequence in which a technician visits addresses
- **Move to a different service day** — shift a customer from Tuesday service to Thursday service
- **Drag to adjust disruption plans** — when the dispatcher proposes a reassignment, override individual stops by dragging them to a different technician

PoolProof recalculates estimated distance, drive time, and completion metrics live as you make each adjustment.

## Multi-Address and Multi-Service-Day Customers

Customers who receive service more than once per week, or who have multiple service addresses with different schedules, are handled automatically. Each address and service frequency appears on the correct route day. The system deduplicates stops so the same address never shows up twice on the same day, and multi-address accounts with different schedules are split correctly across the relevant days.

## Route Performance Tracking

PoolProof tracks actual performance against planned estimates so you can see where routes are accurate and where they need tuning.

| Metric | What It Shows |
|---|---|
| Estimated vs. actual distance | Whether planned mileage reflects reality |
| Estimated vs. actual duration | Whether service time estimates are accurate |
| Completion rate | How reliably stops are being completed on schedule |
| Skip patterns | Which stops are regularly skipped and why |
| Service time accuracy | Which stop types consistently run over or under estimate |

Use this data to refine your route templates over time — tightening estimates, redistributing stops that routinely run long, and catching skip patterns before they become customer complaints.

## Intelligent Message Routing

When a customer replies to an "on the way" notification, that reply needs to reach the technician who is two minutes from the driveway — not sit in a shared office inbox waiting for someone to relay it.

PoolProof's intelligent message routing handles this automatically. When a reply comes in from a customer whose stop is currently in progress or in the active driving window, PoolProof identifies the en-route technician and adds them to the conversation. The tech sees the reply on their phone and can respond directly, without the office ever touching it.

The routing is deliberately precise:

- It only adds the technician who is **genuinely en route** to that specific address
- It adds the tech to the thread — it does not remove existing participants
- Office staff and managers already in the conversation stay on it
- The window opens when the "on the way" notification sends and closes when the visit is marked complete

<Note>
  Intelligent message routing requires SMS notifications to be active. If you haven't set those up yet, go to **Settings → Notifications** and enable SMS Notifications first. Once SMS is running, enable intelligent routing at **Settings → Inbox AI → Intelligent Assignment**.
</Note>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does the optimization algorithm handle customers with multiple service days?">
    Multi-service-day customers are handled automatically. If a customer receives service twice per week, they appear on both route days. The system deduplicates stops within each day and manages multi-address customers with different schedules without any manual setup.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I run optimization on just one route, or does it affect all routes at once?">
    You can optimize individual routes or run optimization across all routes at once. Running it on a single route is useful when you've added a cluster of new customers in one area and want to tighten that day without touching the rest of the week.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to manual adjustments I've made when I run optimization again?">
    Optimization rebuilds routes based on your current customer list and templates. Manual adjustments you've made — such as pinning a customer to a specific technician or day — can be preserved if you've set those constraints before running the optimizer.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does the dispatcher know when a route has drifted?">
    The dispatcher monitors your routes continuously and compares current metrics — total miles, duration, stop counts — against your template baselines. When a route falls meaningfully outside its baseline (extra miles, imbalanced days, one tech consistently finishing late), it surfaces a recommendation to review and rebuild.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does intelligent message routing work with WhatsApp and email, or only SMS?">
    Intelligent message routing currently applies to SMS replies triggered by "on the way" notifications. The broader centralized inbox handles customer conversations across SMS, WhatsApp, and email — and your team can respond to those from a single shared view.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I don't want a customer's reply going to the technician directly?">
    Intelligent Assignment is a global setting you control. If you prefer all customer replies to stay in the shared office inbox, leave the setting off. You can enable or disable it at any time at **Settings → Inbox AI → Intelligent Assignment**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
