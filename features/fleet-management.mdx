PoolProof's fleet management feature gives you complete visibility and control over every vehicle in your operation. From the fleet dashboard to mobile-accessible vehicle photos, this page covers how to track your trucks, stay ahead of maintenance, and keep your team on the road instead of waiting for a tow.

## The Fleet Dashboard

Every vehicle in your fleet appears on the fleet dashboard with its current status and any outstanding maintenance needs. Instead of juggling spreadsheets or trying to remember when the last oil change was, you open one screen and see the full picture — which vehicles are in good shape, which ones have maintenance coming up, and which ones need attention right now.

<Tip>
  Make it a habit to review the fleet dashboard at the start of each week. Catching a brake inspection that's due in 200 miles is far cheaper than dealing with a breakdown mid-route.
</Tip>

## Supported Vehicle Types

PoolProof includes a database of commercial vehicle makes and models so you can set up each truck accurately. Supported types include:

- Pickup trucks
- Cargo vans
- Commercial trucks
- Heavy-duty vehicles from major manufacturers

If you run a specialty vehicle or a model that isn't in the default list, you can enter custom specifications manually. Every vehicle gets its own profile in the system regardless of make or model.

## Vehicle Photos

Document the condition, equipment, and branding of every vehicle with multiple photos per record.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload photos to a vehicle record">
    From the vehicle details page, upload one or more photos — condition shots, equipment layouts, company branding, or anything else you want on file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reorder by drag and drop">
    Arrange photos in any order by dragging them into position. Your preferred lead image shows first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access on mobile">
    Vehicle photos are available in the PoolProof mobile app, so techs and managers can reference them in the field.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Photos are stored securely in PoolProof. Use them to document a vehicle's condition at onboarding, capture equipment installs, or record damage before and after incidents.
</Info>

### Scheduling Triggers

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="By Date">
    Set a maintenance task to trigger on a specific calendar date. Use this for time-based services like annual inspections or seasonal coolant flushes.

    ```text
    Coolant Flush → Due: October 15
    Annual DOT Inspection → Due: March 1
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="By Odometer">
    Set a maintenance task to trigger when the vehicle reaches a target mileage. PoolProof monitors logged odometer readings and alerts you automatically as the vehicle approaches the threshold.

    ```text
    Oil Change → Due at: 52,500 miles (currently at 51,200 miles)
    Tire Rotation → Due at: 55,000 miles
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="By Both">
    Combine date and mileage triggers for whichever comes first — the standard practice for most manufacturer-recommended service intervals.

    ```text
    Oil Change → Due at 52,500 miles OR September 30 — whichever comes first
    ```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Warning>
  Mileage-based alerts only fire accurately if you log odometer readings regularly. Make it part of your end-of-week routine for each vehicle to keep the data current.
</Warning>

### Maintenance Templates

If you run a uniform fleet — or want to apply the same service schedule across all your trucks — create a maintenance template and apply it to every vehicle at once. Set your standard oil change interval, tire rotation schedule, and brake inspection cadence once, then deploy it fleet-wide in seconds.

## Logging Odometer Readings

Accurate mileage tracking is what makes mileage-based maintenance triggers work. Log a reading from any vehicle's details page:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the vehicle details page">
    Select the vehicle from the fleet dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new odometer entry">
    Enter the current mileage reading. Add an optional note — useful for flagging readings taken after a long trip or a particularly demanding week.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review mileage history">
    PoolProof displays all odometer entries for the vehicle over time and automatically calculates miles driven between readings, giving you a clear picture of utilization.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Assign odometer logging to techs at the end of each day or week. A quick entry takes seconds and keeps your maintenance alerts firing at the right time.
</Tip>
