The PoolProof Customer Portal is a fully white-labeled, self-service hub where your customers can view their complete service history, manage invoices, pay balances, and update payment methods — all without picking up the phone. This page covers how the portal works, what customers can do inside it, how to invite customers and manage access, and the business impact you can expect.

## What Customers Can Do

The portal gives every customer a complete, transparent view of their account. They get the answers they need on their own schedule, which means fewer "what happened at my pool?" calls landing in your office.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Service History" icon="clock-rotate-left">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Invoices & Payments" icon="file-invoice-dollar">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Payment Methods" icon="credit-card">

<Tip>
  Customers who can view and pay invoices on their own time pay faster. Giving them self-service access directly shrinks your accounts-receivable cycle without any additional effort from your team.
</Tip>

## 24/7 Access — No Office Hours Required

The portal is always available. Customers who notice a question about last Tuesday's service at 9 PM can log in and get their answer immediately. They don't have to wait until your office opens, and your team doesn't have to field that call the next morning.

This around-the-clock accessibility is one of the most direct ways the portal reduces your inbound support volume.

## Access Methods

Customers can access the portal through two authentication options:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Email & Password">
    The standard login experience. Customers create a password during their first login and use it on every return visit. This works well for customers who prefer a traditional account setup.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Passwordless Magic Links">
    The customer enters their email address and receives a secure, single-use link. Clicking the link logs them straight into the portal — no password to remember or reset. Magic links are convenient for customers who log in infrequently or prefer not to manage another password.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Multiple Users Per Account

Customers can have more than one person access their account. Add additional contacts by email address, and each person gets their own login credentials. Everyone on the same account sees the same service history and billing information, but each user authenticates independently.

This is useful for customers who want a spouse, property manager, or office assistant to have access without sharing login credentials.

## Multiple Service Locations

Customers who own more than one property — a home and a vacation rental, for example — can see each location separately within the same account. Service history, readings, invoices, and communications are organized by property so nothing gets mixed up.

## Customer Feedback

After a service visit, customers can submit feedback directly through the portal. This gives you a structured channel for capturing satisfaction signals and catching issues before they escalate to a review site.

## Your Brand, Not Ours

<Note>
  The Customer Portal is completely white-labeled. Customers see your company name and logo throughout — PoolProof branding does not appear anywhere in the portal experience.
</Note>

From the customer's perspective, the portal is your product. This keeps your brand front and center and reinforces a professional, cohesive experience every time a customer logs in.

## Inviting Customers to the Portal

Send portal invitations directly from a customer's record in PoolProof. The customer receives an email with a link to set up their access.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the customer record">
    Navigate to the customer you want to invite and open their profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the portal invitation">
    Select the option to send a portal invitation. PoolProof emails the customer a secure link to activate their access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customer activates their account">
    The customer clicks the link, chooses their preferred login method (password or magic link), and gains immediate access to their account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  You can send a portal invitation even if no email address is currently on file for the customer. PoolProof will prompt you to add one as part of the invitation flow.
</Info>

## Revoking Access and Resetting Passwords

You stay in control of who has access to the portal. From the customer record, you can revoke portal access for any user at any time. You can also trigger a password reset for a customer who has been locked out or needs to update their credentials.

Both actions take effect immediately and are available directly from the customer profile — no support ticket required.
