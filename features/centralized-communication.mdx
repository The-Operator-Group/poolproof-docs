PoolProof's Centralized Communication feature brings every customer interaction into a single shared inbox — phone calls, texts, emails, website forms, and social media messages — so your whole team works from the same information and nothing falls through the cracks. This page covers the unified inbox, the Service Assistant AI, intelligent message routing, team messaging, and how to configure the settings that power it all.

### Self-Service Booking

When a customer wants to schedule service, the Service Assistant shows your actual available time slots — derived from your routes and technician schedules in real time. The customer picks a slot, provides any necessary details, and the appointment lands on your calendar automatically. No phone tag. No back-and-forth emails. No manual calendar entry.

<Tip>
  The AI only offers times that are genuinely available based on your current routes. Customers can't book into a slot that doesn't exist or is already taken.
</Tip>

### Taking Over a Conversation

Your team can step into any AI-managed conversation at any time. When you reply, the customer sees a continuous thread — there's no awkward handoff message or repeated questions. You see the full history of what the AI already discussed so you have complete context before you type a word.

## Intelligent Message Routing

When a technician is en route to a customer and that customer texts back — "the gate code is 4321" or "can you also check the heater?" — that reply needs to reach the tech, not sit in an office inbox until someone relays it.

PoolProof's Intelligent Assignment feature routes customer replies to the technician who is actively en route to their address. The tech sees the message on their phone, responds directly, and the detail reaches the right person in the moment it matters.

**How routing works:**
- The trigger is an "on the way" notification sent when the tech starts driving to a stop
- From that moment until the visit wraps up, customer replies to that notification route to the en-route tech
- The tech is added to the conversation — existing participants (office staff, managers) stay on the thread
- Routing is based on actual route status, not a guess about who might be nearby

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable SMS Notifications">
    Go to **Settings → Notifications → SMS Notifications** and enable SMS notifications. This is required because Intelligent Assignment depends on the "on the way" message being sent. This is also where your business phone number gets connected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Intelligent Assignment">
    Go to **Settings → Inbox AI** and enable **Intelligent Assignment**. Once it's on, routing happens automatically on every route — there's nothing to configure per technician.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Intelligent Assignment requires SMS notifications to be active. If "on the way" texts aren't going out, replies have no trigger to route from. Set up SMS notifications first before enabling Intelligent Assignment.
</Warning>

## Knowledge Base Management

Control what the Service Assistant knows and how it answers common questions. Navigate to **Settings → Inbox AI** to customize the AI's knowledge base — add details about your service offerings, pricing, service area policies, FAQs, and any other information that helps the AI represent your business accurately.

Keeping the knowledge base current means the AI gives accurate answers and escalates less frequently. The better the information you provide, the more autonomously the AI can handle routine inquiries.

## Team Messaging

PoolProof includes messaging between individuals and groups on your team. Use it for internal coordination — route changes, equipment notes, scheduling updates — without leaving PoolProof to switch to a separate messaging app. Team messages are separate from customer conversations and stay internal.
