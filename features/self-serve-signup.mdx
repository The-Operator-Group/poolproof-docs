PoolProof's Self-Serve Signup turns your website into a round-the-clock sales channel. Prospects can select a service plan, enter their property information, enroll in autopay, and sign your service agreement entirely online — without calling your office, waiting for a callback, or chasing down paperwork. This page explains how the signup flow works, how service area validation protects your time, what happens after a prospect submits, and how to share the signup page with prospects.

## How It Works

The signup page is a single, guided flow that collects everything you need to start a new customer relationship — before your team ever gets involved.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Address validation">
    The prospect enters their service address first. PoolProof checks it against your configured service areas immediately. Customers within your coverage area proceed to plan selection. Customers outside your coverage receive a friendly message or get flagged for manual review, depending on your preference — so they're never left wondering what happened.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan selection">
    The prospect chooses from your available service plans. They see the options you've configured, priced the way you've set them up — no guesswork and no phone calls to figure out what service they need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customer information">
    The prospect provides their contact details, property specifics, and any other information your signup form requests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payment method & autopay enrollment">
    The prospect enters a payment method and enrolls in autopay as part of signup. Their billing is set up before the first service visit — no chasing a card number after the fact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Service agreement signature">
    The prospect reviews your service agreement and signs it digitally before completing signup. The signed agreement is stored automatically and is legally binding. No PDFs to email, no printing, no scanning, no chasing signatures.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Conditional confirmation">
    The prospect receives a confirmation that their signup is received. Service does not begin automatically — their account is pending staff verification and approval.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Service Area Validation

<Note>
  Address validation happens at the very beginning of the signup flow — before the prospect invests time entering their information. This protects both parties: prospects outside your area find out immediately, and your team never receives a complete signup for an address you can't serve.
</Note>

You configure your service areas in PoolProof, and the signup page enforces them automatically. When an address falls outside your coverage:

- **Friendly rejection:** The prospect sees a message explaining you don't currently serve their area.
- **Manual review flag:** Alternatively, the signup can be flagged for your team to review — useful for edge cases near your service area boundaries where you might want to make an exception.

Choose the behavior that fits your business in your service area settings.

## Staff Verification Before Service Begins

Every signup is conditionally approved. A customer completing the signup form does not automatically become an active customer on your route. Service begins only after your staff reviews and approves the signup.

<Accordion title="What does staff verification involve?">
  Your team reviews the submitted customer information, conducts a virtual assessment using the details the customer provided, and confirms the selected service plan is the right fit for the property. Once everything checks out, you approve the signup and service can be scheduled.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if we need to reject a signup?">
  If verification reveals a problem — the address is actually outside your service area, the pool's condition is beyond the scope of the selected plan, or any other issue — you can cancel the signup before service begins. The customer is notified, and no service visit is ever scheduled. You stay in full control of who you accept.
</Accordion>

<Warning>
  Never skip the verification step. Conditional approval exists to protect your business. A few minutes of review prevents the much larger cost of showing up to a property that isn't a good fit.
</Warning>
