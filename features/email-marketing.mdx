## Building an Email Campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Email Marketing section">
    Navigate to **Email Marketing** in your PoolProof sidebar and click **New Campaign**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose or create a template">
    Select from included templates — seasonal openings, service reminders, add-on promotions, and customer appreciation messages — or start from a blank canvas in the drag-and-drop builder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize your content">
    Add your messaging, images, and a call to action. The preview pane shows exactly how the email renders on desktop and mobile before you send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define your segment">
    Use the segmentation panel to filter your recipient list. Combine multiple conditions with AND/OR logic, then click **Preview Recipients** to confirm the customer count before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your reply-to address">
    Choose the reply-to address for this campaign. Replies route to your unified inbox regardless of which address you select.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send immediately or schedule">
    Send the campaign now, or pick a future date and time for delivery. Scheduled campaigns run automatically without any additional action from your team.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Segmentation: Sending to the Right Customers

Sending the same message to every customer rarely works. A filter cleaning promotion is relevant to customers with cartridge filters — not necessarily everyone on your list. PoolProof's segmentation lets you build precise recipient lists based on the data already in your account.

### Available Segment Filters

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Location and Service Area">
    Filter by **service area**, **city**, **zip code**, or **route**. Use this to target a promotion to one geographic region, notify customers in a specific area about a schedule change, or run a local referral campaign.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Service Schedule">
    Filter by **service day** (Monday customers only, for example) or by **last service date**. Useful for reaching customers who haven't had a visit recently, or targeting a specific day's customers for a schedule notice.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Customer Status and Plan">
    Filter by **customer status** (active, inactive, on hold) or by **service plan**. Target customers on your basic plan with an upgrade offer, or reach only active customers for a loyalty promotion.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Billing and Payment">
    Filter by **billing method**, **card on file** status, or **account created date**. Useful for campaigns encouraging customers to add a card on file, or for reaching long-time customers with an anniversary offer.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Always click **Preview Recipients** before sending a campaign. This shows you the exact customer list your filters produce so you can confirm you're reaching the right people.
</Tip>

### AND/OR Logic

You can combine multiple filters to narrow or broaden your list:

- **AND logic** narrows the list — a customer must match all conditions to be included.
- **OR logic** broadens the list — a customer matching any condition is included.

For example: customers on the **Premium Plan** AND located in **ZIP code 78209** AND with **no card on file** — a focused segment for a targeted payment-enrollment push.

## Transactional Email Customization

Beyond marketing campaigns, PoolProof automatically sends transactional emails on your behalf: quote deliveries, invoice notifications, service completion summaries, and payment receipts. All of these are customizable.

<Note>
  Transactional emails are sent from your whitelabeled domain and reflect your branding. Customize the subject lines, body copy, and visual style to match your company's voice — not a generic template.
</Note>

To customize a transactional email, go to **Settings > Email Templates** and select the template you want to edit. Changes apply to all future sends of that email type.

**Customizable transactional emails include:**

- Quote delivery emails
- Invoice notifications
- Service completion notices (includes technician notes, chemical readings, and photos)
- Payment receipts
- Overdue payment reminders

## How Replies Work

When a customer replies to one of your campaigns, their message lands in your PoolProof unified inbox — the same inbox where you see SMS messages, WhatsApp conversations, and other customer communications.

This matters for two reasons:

1. **You won't miss a lead.** A customer who replies "Can you add a heater service?" to your seasonal email is a sales opportunity. That opportunity shows up in your inbox immediately, not buried in a separate marketing tool.
2. **Your team handles everything in one place.** There's no need to check a separate marketing platform for campaign replies. All customer communication — regardless of channel or how it started — is in one inbox.
