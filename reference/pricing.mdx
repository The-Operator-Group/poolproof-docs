## White-Glove Onboarding and Migration (Included)

Every PoolProof subscription includes white-glove onboarding and migration — a **$5,000 value** — at no additional cost.

PoolProof assigns you a dedicated team member who handles the entire transition:

- **Data migration** — imports your customer records, service history, routes, and billing data from your current system
- **Configuration** — sets up PoolProof to match how your business actually operates: service plans, areas, workflows, billing rules
- **Team training** — walks your staff through the platform before you go live

Most customers are fully operational in **30 days or less**. You don't need to prep spreadsheets, fill out templates, or manage the migration yourself.

<Info>
  PoolProof does the migration work before you commit. You can log in, review your own customers and routes inside the platform, and decide with real information — not a promise.
</Info>

## Full Feature List

Every feature below is included in both the Annual and Monthly plans.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Customer Management">
    - **Multiple Service Addresses** — Store multiple service locations per customer
    - **Service Completion Notices** — Automatically email customers after each visit with technician notes, photos, and recommendations
    - **Access Information** — Store gate codes, lockbox codes, and property access details per service address
    - **Customer Notes** — Record notes visible to technicians, sales, and back-office staff
    - **Multiple Bodies of Water** — Support multiple pools or spas per location, each with its own service plan
    - **Customer Portal** — Let customers view invoices, make payments, update payment methods, and schedule services online
    - **Self-Serve Customer Signup** — Allow residential customers to sign up for recurring service online; validates service area, selects service day, and handles contract execution
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Routing and Scheduling">
    - **Digital Dispatcher** — AI-powered routing assistant that continuously analyzes routes, handles disruptions, monitors weather, and surfaces optimization recommendations
    - **Optimize Routes** — Reduce drive time between stops with one-click route optimization
    - **Real-Time Progress Updates** — Track technician progress and stop completion status as service happens
    - **Route Templates** — Versioned templates to track changes and evaluate different routing decisions
    - **Show Nearest Routes** — Identify the best route assignment when adding or modifying a customer's schedule
    - **Filter Clean Auto Scheduling** — Automatically schedule filter cleaning jobs at configured intervals
    - **Salt Cell Clean Auto Scheduling** — Automatically schedule salt cell cleaning at configured intervals
    - **Equipment Testing Auto Scheduling** — Set periodic equipment testing tasks to ensure optimal performance
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Email Marketing">
    - **Campaigns** — Send targeted messages to customer segments with integrated engagement tracking
    - **Segmentation** — Filter recipients by service area, service day, customer status, service plan, city, zip code, billing method, route, and more
    - **Drag-and-Drop Template Builder** — Create and manage professional email templates without design skills
    - **Whitelabeled Sending Domains** — Send campaigns from your own domain to protect sender reputation
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Centralized Communication">
    - **Unified Inbox** — One shared inbox for every customer interaction, accessible by your entire team
    - **Service Assistant** — AI assistant that handles customer conversations across SMS, WhatsApp, email, and more; answers routine questions and escalates when a human is needed
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Service Plans and Areas">
    - **Service Plans** — Create plans assigned to bodies of water that define billing, workflows, and periodic maintenance — ensuring consistent service delivery without per-customer configuration
    - **Service Areas** — Define territories you service; assign customers and technicians to areas
    - **Multiple Price Points per Plan** — Support advance/arrears billing, per-visit or per-month pricing, chemical usage charges, and automatic tax calculation
    - **Configurable Reading Requirements** — Set chemical reading requirements as optional or required at a plan-defined frequency
    - **Filter PSI Recording** — Capture filter PSI values to monitor system health and filter performance
    - **Process Workflows** — Define tasks required before, during, or after service
    - **Flexible Process Responses** — Tasks can be marked complete, answered via free text, multiple choice, or yes/no
    - **Photo Requirements** — Require photos as part of specific task completions
    - **Video Capture** — Capture video as part of a task completion step
    - **Periodic Maintenance** — Ensure maintenance tasks (backwashing, filter cleanings, salt cell cleaning) occur at scheduled intervals; configurable as in-service tasks or independent jobs
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Quoting and Work Orders">
    - **Create and Manage Quotes** — Full quote lifecycle management in web and mobile; supports photo/PDF attachments and line-item tags
    - **Create and Manage Work Orders** — Full work order lifecycle in web and mobile
    - **Customer Quote Approval** — Require customers to review and approve quotes before work begins
    - **Automated Work Order Creation** — Work orders are automatically created when a quote is approved
    - **Management Approval Thresholds** — Require internal review for all quotes or only those above a dollar threshold
    - **Discounting** — Apply percentage-based discounts across all line items
    - **Pre-Payment Requirements** — Require full or partial pre-payment before work begins
    - **Follow-Up Notifications** — Automatic reminders to customers and quote owners when a quote needs action
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Product Catalog">
    - **Products** — Create and manage products added to quotes, work orders, and invoices
    - **Services** — Create and manage services added to quotes, work orders, and invoices
    - **Categories** — Organize products and services into categories
    - **Inventory Management** — Manage stocked, as-needed, or manually tracked inventory; set reorder thresholds per service area
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Equipment Management">
    - **Detailed Equipment Profiles** — Record pool and spa configuration for each body of water: sanitation type and method, filter type, heating method, automation system
    - **Pool and Spa Notes** — Notes displayed to technicians during service
    - **Tailored Workflows** — Service workflows automatically adjust based on equipment type and sanitation method
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Billing">
    - **Automatic Invoicing** — Invoices created automatically for recurring services and completed work orders
    - **Automated Collections** — When a payment method is on file, collections run fully automatically
    - **Smart Retries** — Failed payments automatically retry when a card is most likely to have funds
    - **Automatic Card Updates** — Cards updated automatically when reissued (new expiration dates, replacement cards)
    - **Bank Drafts (ACH)** — Customers can pay via ACH to reduce processing fees
    - **Sales Tax Calculations** — Tax calculated automatically based on billing address; no tax tables to manage; supports tax-exempt organizations
    - **Refunds** — Issue partial or full refunds on any invoice
    - **Credits** — Apply credits that reduce future invoice amounts
    - **QuickBooks Integration** — Sync invoices, payments, refunds, and credits to QuickBooks Online in real time
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Authentication and Access Control">
    - **Role-Based Access Control** — Assign roles that match job functions and limit access accordingly
    - **Multi-Organization Support** — Users can belong to and switch between multiple organizations
    - **Google Authentication** — Allow or require team members to sign in with their Google account
    - **Microsoft Authentication** — Allow or require team members to sign in with their Microsoft account
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
