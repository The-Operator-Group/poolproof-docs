PoolProof ships product updates on a continuous weekly cadence, driven by real feedback from pool service businesses. This changelog tracks every release — new features, improvements, and fixes — organized by release week and product area so you always know what changed and when.

---

### September 6 – September 12, 2026
**A new invoicing flow, and the mobile app in Spanish**

- **Invoicing** — Rebuilt create-invoice flow and a redesigned invoice details view
- **QuickBooks** — Processing fees and a clearing account structure for clean reconciliation
- **Mobile** — Service visit report is now one scrollable screen instead of a series of steps
- **Spanish** — The mobile application now supports Spanish translations throughout
- **Work Orders** — Reorder template items into the sequence technicians actually follow
- **Email Marketing** — Choose the reply-to address on campaign emails

---

### August 30 – September 5, 2026
**More flexible route scheduling and a new route mileage report**

- **Routes** — Move a route to another service week, with smarter bi-weekly scheduling behind it
- **Routes** — Reschedule a skipped stop instead of losing the visit
- **Reports** — A new Route Mileage report, with filters and sorting that survive the back button
- **Email Notifications** — New invoice and payment merge tags for businesses that collect payment later
- **QuickBooks** — A price point can bill under the service you choose in QuickBooks
- **Service** — Last backwash, filter clean, and salt cell clean dates are tracked per pool
- **Mobile** — The schedule tab shows total, completed, and skipped stop counts at a glance

### August 23 – August 29, 2026
**Enhancements to the mobile application for more reliable field operations**

- **Mobile** — Re-designed route flow to work more reliably when connectivity is poor
- **Mobile** — Photos and actions upload through a background queue that survives closing the app (Android)
- **Dashboard** — Review skipped stops and recover any of them as a one-time visit
- **Routes** — Reassign stops to technicians filtered by skill, with every change recorded
- **Work Orders / Quotes** — The add-item dialog works like a basket — build the list, then add it in one go
