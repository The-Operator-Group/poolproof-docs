## Migration

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I switch pool service software mid-season?">
    You can, but avoid it if you have a choice. The safest time to migrate is during the **shoulder season** — after the summer rush, before spring opening. Cut at the end of a billing cycle and a service week to minimize disruption.

    If you must move mid-season, complete your data exports and resolve payment method handling before your next billing run. PoolProof's done-for-you migration significantly reduces the burden, but timing still matters.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will I lose my service history when I switch?">
    Not necessarily. PoolProof migrates your customer records and service history as part of white-glove onboarding. Most data transfers successfully — including readings, visit history, notes, and routes.

    The two things that most often don't survive a migration automatically are **photos and attachments** (which don't live in a CSV export) and **saved payment authorizations** (which are vaulted with the payment processor, not the software). PoolProof will walk you through both during onboarding.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do my customers' saved credit cards transfer?">
    Often not automatically. Your customers' cards are vaulted with the payment processor — not stored in the software itself. When you switch software, you may also be switching processors, and card tokens don't automatically follow.

    In some cases a compliant vault-to-vault transfer is possible. If it isn't, you'll need to re-collect payment details from autopay customers. Ask PoolProof about your specific processor situation early in the process — before you've committed to anything — so you can plan accordingly.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How long does migration take?">
    **30 days or less** with PoolProof's done-for-you migration service. Because PoolProof handles the data work — no templates for you to fill out, no spreadsheets to clean up — most of your time is spent reviewing the results and getting your team trained, not doing the migration itself.

    Self-service migrations at other vendors typically take one to three weeks of your own hours, often competing with running your business in season.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Features

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does PoolProof have a mobile app?">
    Yes. PoolProof has native apps for both **iOS** and **Android**. Technicians use the mobile app in the field to view assigned routes, execute service stops, log chemical readings, complete task checklists, capture photos, and access customer notes and equipment details.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What accounting software does PoolProof integrate with?">
    PoolProof integrates with **QuickBooks Online**. Invoices, payments, refunds, and credits sync to QuickBooks in real time — so your books stay current without manual data entry.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which communication channels does PoolProof support?">
    PoolProof's unified inbox handles customer messages from:

    - **SMS** (text messaging)
    - **WhatsApp**
    - **Email**
    - **Facebook Messages**
    - **Website contact forms**

    All of these land in a single shared inbox your entire team can access. No separate tools, no missed messages across channels.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will AI respond to every customer message automatically?">
    Routine messages get instant automatic responses from the Service Assistant — things like service day questions, pricing inquiries, and common requests. These are answered immediately using your actual customer data.

    Complex or unusual situations — complaints, requests that require judgment, anything that warrants your personal attention — get flagged for your team with the full conversation context already gathered. You remain in control of every significant customer interaction.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I adjust routes after they've been optimized?">
    Yes. Optimization is a starting point, not a locked decision. You can drag stops to reorder them, move a stop to a different day, or reassign stops to a different technician. All changes take effect immediately and are reflected in the mobile app for your technicians.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
