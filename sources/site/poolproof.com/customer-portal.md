## Frequently Asked Questions

[Contact Us](https://poolproof.com/get-in-touch/)

How do customers access the portal?

Customers can log in with email and password or use passwordless magic links. Magic links send a secure link to their email. Click to access, no password required.

Can multiple people access the same customer account?

Yes. Additional users can be added by email address. Each person gets their own login credentials while seeing the same service history and billing information.

Can customers pay invoices through the portal?

Yes. Customers can view invoices, pay outstanding balances, and download invoice PDFs directly from the portal.

Can customers update their credit card?

Yes. Customers can add new cards, remove old cards, and set their default payment method through self-service.

Does the portal show PoolProof branding?

No. The Customer Portal is completely white-labeled. Customers see your company logo and name, not PoolProof.
