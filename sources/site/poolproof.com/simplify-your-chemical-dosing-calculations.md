Chemical dosing is one of the most repeated parts of your day, and for most teams, it is still handled manually.

At every stop, your techs test the water, review the readings, and determine what needs to be added to bring the pool back into balance. Even when using tools like Orenda, those calculations still have to be done over and over throughout the day. While your software may help track the service, it usually does not support the actual chemistry decisions happening at each pool.

After a full day of stops, that repetition adds up. It slows your team down, breaks their rhythm, and creates a constant point of friction in the work.

## **The Real Problem Isn’t the Math. It’s the Repetition.**

We know the math itself is not what slows you down. It is the fact that your techs have to stop and think through it at every pool. Test, read, decide. Over and over again.

It might only take a couple of minutes at each stop, but across a full route, that time adds up. It slows your techs down, cuts into productivity, and puts pressure on margin. Time is money in this business.

And when the same decision has to be made over and over, mistakes start to happen. Small miscalculations, inconsistent adjustments, and issues that should not be issues. That is where the real problem is.

## **What This Should Look Like Instead**

By the time your tech enters readings, the system should already know what to do.

It has the pool volume, your target ranges, the current readings, and the LSI. There should not be another step where your tech has to stop and figure it out. The dosage should already be there.

So instead of calculating at every stop, your tech is reviewing and confirming. No second guessing, no slowing down, and no unnecessary room for error. Just automated chemical dosing built into the workflow.

## **How PoolProof Changes the Workflow**

PoolProof builds chemical dosing directly into the service workflow, so your team can move through each stop without slowing down.

Here’s what that looks like in practice. Your tech arrives at a stop, tests the water, and enters the readings into PoolProof. The system already has the pool’s volume, your configured target ranges, and the Langelier Saturation Index built in. As soon as the readings are entered, PoolProof returns a dosing recommendation that shows exactly what to add and how much, using only the chemicals you’ve set up. Your tech reviews it, makes any adjustments if needed, and completes the service.

The readings and dosage are logged automatically, so there is no separate calculation step, no chart to reference, and no need to rely on memory. The whole process takes seconds, and every stop is recorded the same way.

## **Consistency Across Every Tech**

Manual dosing leads to small differences between techs, and those differences add up.

When dosing is built into the system, everyone follows the same logic and the same process. There is no guessing and no doing things differently from one tech to the next.

That means more consistent water, fewer mistakes, and a clearer standard across your team as you grow.

## **Built for How Your Business Actually Runs**

Proper dosing is one of the most important parts of every service stop, and getting it right cannot be left to guesswork. Your team needs an easier way to calculate dosing and a more reliable way to record readings right at the stop.

That is exactly what PoolProof is built for. It helps your team handle a critical part of the job with more consistency, less friction, and far less manual work behind the scenes.
