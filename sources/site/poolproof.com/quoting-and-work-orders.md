## About Quoting and Work Orders

[Get a Demo](https://poolproof.com/schedule-a-demo/)

##### **Smart Scheduling Engine**

Double bookings. Unqualified techs on specialized jobs. No travel time between stops. These mistakes cost you time and customer trust.

PoolProof detects conflicts before they happen, enforces buffers between appointments, and matches jobs to techs based on skills. The right person on the right job, every time.

##### **Approvals That Don’t Slow You Down**

Not every quote needs review, but some definitely do. PoolProof routes quotes based on your rules: big discounts go to a manager, routine quotes go straight to customers.

No chasing signatures on small jobs. No unauthorized discounts slipping through. You define the rules; the system enforces them.

##### **Faster Setup for Repeat Services**

Filter cleanings. Equipment inspections. Seasonal openings. Instead of rebuilding these work orders from scratch, use templates with pre-filled line items, skills, and durations. What took 10 minutes now takes 30 seconds.

Update templates anytime. Changes apply to future jobs without touching completed work.

## Frequently Asked Questions

[Contact Us](https://poolproof.com/get-in-touch/)

What happens when a customer accepts a quote?

The quote status updates to “Approved” and you’re notified immediately. From there, the quote is converted to a work order. All line items, customer details, and pricing transfer automatically.

Can I require approval for certain quotes?

Yes. Configure approval workflows based on discount levels, quote amounts, or other criteria. Quotes meeting your thresholds route to designated approvers before going to customers.

How does scheduling work for work orders?

PoolProof shows real-time technician availability with automatic conflict detection. The system enforces 30-minute buffers between appointments for travel time and matches technicians based on required skills.

Can technicians access work orders on mobile?

Yes. The mobile app gives technicians full access to their assigned work orders with customer details, line items, notes, and the ability to update status in real-time.

Is there an audit trail for quotes?

Yes. Every status change, approval, and customer response is logged with timestamps and actor identification. You have complete visibility into who did what and when.
