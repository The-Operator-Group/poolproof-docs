## Frequently Asked Questions

[Contact Us](https://poolproof.com/get-in-touch/)

How does route optimization work?

PoolProof uses a multi-stage algorithm. First, it groups customers by service day. Then it clusters nearby customers geographically. Next, it balances routes by duration. Finally, it consolidates undersized routes. The result is efficient routes that make sense in the real world.

Can I adjust routes after optimization?

Absolutely. Optimization gives you a starting point, but you have full control. Drag stops between routes, reorder within a route, or move customers to different service days. The system recalculates metrics as you edit.

What about customers with multiple service days?

They are handled automatically. If a customer receives service twice per week, they appear on both routes. The system deduplicates and handles multi-address customers with different schedules.

How do technicians access their routes?

The mobile app shows each technician their assigned route with stops in order, customer details, and one-tap navigation. Changes made in the office sync automatically.

Can I track route performance?

Yes. PoolProof tracks estimated vs. actual distance, duration, and completion rates. See which routes consistently run over or under, identify skip patterns, and measure service time accuracy.
