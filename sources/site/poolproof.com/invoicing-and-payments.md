## About Invoicing and Payments

[Get a Demo](https://poolproof.com/schedule-a-demo/)

##### **Flexible Billing**

Every pool service company bills differently, and software shouldn’t force you to change. PoolProof lets you bill per visit or monthly, in advance or arrears, with chemicals included in your service price or itemized separately. Set payment terms that match how you operate: due immediately, NET 30, NET 60 or custom terms. Your pricing model, your rules.

##### **Automatic Collections**

Customers pay automatically via credit card or ACH without you lifting a finger. When a payment fails, PoolProof doesn’t just notify you and leave you to chase it down; it automatically retries the payment on a smart schedule and notifies the customer directly. You stay out of the awkward collection conversation entirely.

##### **Tax Management**

Pool routes don’t follow tax boundaries. Your Tuesday route might cross three different cities with three different rates. PoolProof automatically calculates the correct sales tax for each service address based on location, so you’re not guessing or manually looking up rates. Mark commercial accounts or tax-exempt customers with a single setting.

##### **QuickBooks Online Integration**

Invoices, payments, refunds, and sales tax data sync automatically to QuickBooks Online as transactions occur. No copying invoice numbers into spreadsheets, no reconciling mismatched totals, no end-of-month data entry marathon. Your books stay accurate in real time, and month-end close becomes a non-event.

##### **Keep More Revenue**

Credit card processing fees quietly drain 2-3% of every payment. For a company collecting $40k monthly, that’s $800-1,200 walking out the door. PoolProof lets you pass processing costs to customers automatically with compliant surcharges. Funds typically hit your account within a few days, and faster payouts are available for qualified customers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

[Contact Us](https://poolproof.com/get-in-touch/)

How do customers receive invoices?

Invoices can be delivered via email or SMS. Each invoice includes your company logo and a direct link to a payment portal where customers can view their balance and pay immediately.

What happens when a credit card fails?

PoolProof automatically retries failed payments on a smart schedule and sends the customer a notification to update their payment method. You don’t have to make the awkward follow-up call.

What are credit card processing fees?

Every time a customer pays by credit card, payment processors charge a fee to handle the transaction. Pool Proof uses Stripe’s standard pricing: 2.9% + $0.30 when customers enter their own card through the payment portal, or 3.4% + $0.30 when your office manually keys in a card number. ACH bank transfers are 0.8%, capped at $5. On a $150 monthly service bill, that’s roughly $4.65 via customer-entered card, $5.40 if your office enters it, or $1.20 via ACH. This is why encouraging customers to set up autopay themselves or ACH can save you money over time.

How quickly do I get paid?

Funds typically hit your account within a few days. Faster payouts are available for qualified customers.

What accounting software do you integrate with?

PoolProof currently integrates with QuickBooks Online. Invoices, payments, refunds, and sales tax data sync automatically.
