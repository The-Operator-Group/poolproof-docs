## Frequently Asked Questions

[Contact Us](https://poolproof.com/get-in-touch/)

Will the AI respond to every message automatically?

The AI handles routine inquiries instantly: service questions, pricing requests, scheduling, and basic information. For complex situations, unusual requests, or anything that needs human judgment, the conversation gets flagged for your team. You stay in control of the conversations that matter while the AI handles the volume that would otherwise overwhelm you.

What happens if the AI doesn't know the answer?

When the AI encounters something outside its knowledge, it acknowledges the question and lets the customer know a team member will follow up. The conversation gets flagged in your inbox so nothing falls through the cracks. Your customers get a response within seconds, and you get the context you need to help them.

How does self-service booking work?

When customers want to schedule service, the AI shows them your actual availability based on your routes and technician schedules. They pick a time that works, provide the necessary details, and the appointment gets added to your calendar automatically. No phone tag. No back-and-forth emails. Just booked appointments appearing on your schedule.

Which communication channels are supported?

All of them. Phone calls, texts, emails, website forms, and social media messages flow into a single inbox. Your customers reach out however they prefer, and you see everything in one place. No more checking five different apps to make sure you haven’t missed something.

What if I want to jump into a conversation the AI started?

Take over anytime. The AI hands off seamlessly when you want to respond personally. The customer sees a continuous conversation. You see the full context of what’s already been discussed. There’s no awkward transition or repeated questions.
