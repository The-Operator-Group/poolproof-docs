## Frequently Asked Questions

[Contact Us](https://poolproof.com/get-in-touch/)

What's the difference between service plan processes and work order processes?

Service plan processes guide recurring visits (weekly cleanings, chemical service) with phase-organized workflows and completion tracking. Work order processes enforce one-time jobs (repairs, installations, warranty work) with required steps that must be completed before closing.

Where do I define processes?

Service plan processes are defined on each service plan, so different service levels can have different processes. Work order processes are defined on templates, and every work order created from a template inherits its process.

What's the difference between the three phases?

Before Service captures site conditions and equipment status before work begins. During Service covers chemical testing, treatment, and cleaning tasks. After Service handles final documentation, photos, and completion verification. This mirrors how experienced technicians naturally approach a job.

What input types are supported?

Service plan processes support checklists, yes/no, text response, multiple choice, chemical readings, and optional photo capture. Work order processes add serial number entry, barcode/QR scanning, numeric inputs, before/after photo pairs, and customer and technician signature capture.
