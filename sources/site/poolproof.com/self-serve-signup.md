##### **Online Signup: Self-Service Customer Acquisition**

PoolProof’s Online Signup turns your website into a 24/7 sales channel. Prospective customers enter their information, select their service plan, provide payment, and sign agreements online. Your team receives a complete signup ready for verification. No phone tag. No paperwork chase. No lost opportunities.

##### **Digital Agreements, Signed and Stored**

Customers review and sign your service agreement as part of the signup process. No emailing PDFs. No printing and scanning. No chasing signatures. Agreement signed digitally, stored automatically, legally binding.

##### **Only Accept Customers You Can Serve**

Before customers invest time in signup, the system validates their address against your service areas. Customers within coverage proceed to plan selection. Customers outside coverage receive appropriate handling. No wasted time for anyone.

## Frequently Asked Questions

[Contact Us](https://poolproof.com/get-in-touch/)

How do customers access the signup page?

The signup page is a standalone URL you can link from your website, share in advertising, or provide to prospects directly. It’s also embeddable in your existing website.

What if a customer's address isn't in our service area?

Addresses outside your configured service areas can be rejected with a friendly message or flagged for manual review, depending on your preference.

Are signups automatically approved?

Signups are conditionally approved. Customers receive confirmation, but service doesn’t begin until your staff reviews and verifies the signup.

What does staff verification involve?

Staff reviews the customer information, conducts a virtual assessment using provided details, confirms the service plan fit, and approves the signup.

Can we reject a signup after submission?

Yes. If verification reveals issues (wrong service area, pool conditions beyond scope, etc.), staff can cancel before service begins.
