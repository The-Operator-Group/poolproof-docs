## Frequently Asked Questions

[Contact Us](https://poolproof.com/get-in-touch/)

Do I need design experience to create emails?

No. The visual email builder is drag-and-drop. Add text, images, buttons, and layouts by clicking and dragging. No HTML or design skills required. Preview your email before sending to see exactly what customers will receive.

How do I choose who receives a campaign?

Create segments based on customer attributes: service area, service day, customer status, city, zip code, service plan, or dates (created date, last service date). Combine multiple filters with AND/OR logic. Preview matching customers before sending.

What happens when a customer replies to a campaign?

Replies come into your unified inbox alongside texts, WhatsApp messages, and other customer communications. You won’t miss a lead or service request because it’s buried in a separate marketing tool.

Can I customize the system emails (quotes, invoices, etc.)?

Yes. Transactional email templates can be customized with your branding and messaging. Modify the defaults to match your company’s voice and appearance.

Do I need a separate email marketing subscription?

No. Email marketing is included in PoolProof. You don’t need Mailchimp, Constant Contact, or any other service.
