## About Service Plans and Areas

[Get a Demo](https://poolproof.com/schedule-a-demo/)

##### **Process Steps Tied to Plans**

Each service plan includes a defined process with the steps technicians follow during every visit. A basic plan might have a streamlined process. A premium plan might include detailed equipment inspection steps. The plan determines the process, and the process ensures consistency.

##### **Maintenance Schedules Built In**

Filter cleans, equipment inspections, seasonal prep. These tasks don’t happen every visit, and they’re easy to forget. Maintenance items let you define periodic tasks per plan with their own schedules. The system tracks what’s due and ensures nothing falls through the cracks.

##### **Flexible Pricing Within Plans**

The same service definition can have multiple price points. Charge more in premium neighborhoods. Offer introductory rates for new customers. Create commercial pricing separate from residential. Different prices, same underlying service specification.

## Frequently Asked Questions

[Contact Us](https://poolproof.com/get-in-touch/)

How many service plans can I create?

Unlimited. Create as many plans as your business needs with different tiers, specialized services, or seasonal offerings.

Can one plan have multiple prices?

Yes. Price points let you create unlimited pricing variations for the same plan. Different prices for neighborhoods, customer types, or promotions, all tied to the same service definition.

How do plans connect to processes?

Each plan includes a defined process with steps technicians follow during visits. Different plans can have different processes matching their service level.

What are maintenance items?

Periodic tasks that don’t happen every visit. Filter cleaning, equipment inspection, seasonal prep. Configure frequency and whether the task requires a separate appointment.

How do chemical settings work per plan?

Each plan configures which readings technicians capture and which chemicals are used for dosing. Set automated dosing, manual dosing, or monitoring-only per reading type.
