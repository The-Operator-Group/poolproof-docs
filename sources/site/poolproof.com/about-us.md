## Our Story

[How We Do It Better](https://poolproof.com/compare/)

The Beginning

PoolProof is built by The Operator Group. We find industries where business owners are overcharged for software that underdelivers, and we build something better. Our founders have built and scaled companies from the ground up, leading teams through the messy middle stages of growth where operations either come together or fall apart. That experience is baked into everything we build.

We’re Not Pool People

We’ve never run a pool service company. We think that’s an advantage.

We came into this industry without legacy assumptions about how things should work. Instead, we spent months talking to pool service owners. We asked what was broken, what was missing, and what they wished their software actually did.

Every feature in PoolProof started as a conversation with a real operator. We don’t build what we think is clever. We build what actually solves problems for the people doing the work every day.

We Chose This Industry On Purpose

Pool service businesses are run by smart operators who deserve better tools. But most of the software in this space was built to track work, not to actually help you run your company. We saw an opportunity to build something that goes further—software that automates the work your office manager, dispatcher, and bookkeeper are doing manually today.

We’re here because we believe pool service companies are exactly the kind of businesses that benefit most from intelligent automation, and nobody was building it for you.

We’re Not Beholden To Venture Capital

Many great companies wouldn’t exist without venture capital. But every dollar raised comes with the expectation of exponential growth, and that pressure changes how companies operate. They overhire, spend aggressively to win market share, and eventually, customers end up paying more for a product that’s getting worse, not better.

We built PoolProof differently. With today’s tools, you don’t need armies of engineers and salespeople to build great software. You need a small team that’s obsessively focused on the product.

We’re building a company that can deliver better software at a lower cost, and stick around long enough for you to depend on it.
