## Frequently Asked Questions

[Contact Us](https://poolproof.com/get-in-touch/)

What maintenance tasks can I schedule?

PoolProof includes common task types: Oil Change, Tire Rotation, Brake Inspection, Air Filter, Transmission Service, Coolant Flush, Battery Check, Belt Inspection, Fuel Filter, and Spark Plugs. You can also create custom tasks with any name you choose.

Can I schedule maintenance by mileage?

Yes. Maintenance tasks can be scheduled by date, by odometer reading, or both. “Oil change at 50,000 miles” works just as well as “Oil change on March 15th.” When you log mileage approaching the target, alerts trigger automatically.

How do I log odometer readings?

From the vehicle details page, add a new reading with the current mileage and an optional note. The system tracks all readings over time and calculates miles driven between entries.

Can I upload photos of vehicles?

Yes. Upload multiple photos per vehicle to document condition, equipment, branding, or anything else. Photos are stored securely and can be reordered by drag-and-drop.

What vehicle types are supported?

PoolProof includes a database of commercial vehicle makes and models, including pickup trucks, cargo vans, commercial trucks, and heavy-duty vehicles from major manufacturers. Select from the list or enter custom specifications.
