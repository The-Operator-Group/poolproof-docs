## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Schedule a Demo">
    Every PoolProof account starts with a personal onboarding call. [Book a time](https://poolproof.com/schedule-a-demo) with the PoolProof team to see the platform and discuss your operation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="White-Glove Migration">
    The PoolProof team migrates your customers, routes, service history, and billing data from your current platform — before you sign anything. You review your real business inside PoolProof before committing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Your Operation">
    Set up service areas, plans, pricing, and workflows to match how your business actually runs. See [Account Setup](/getting-started/account-setup) for a full walkthrough.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go Live">
    Most customers go live in 30 days or less. Your team downloads the mobile app, techs get their routes, and PoolProof starts doing the work.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  PoolProof does not yet support self-service account creation. Every customer is onboarded personally to ensure PoolProof is configured for your workflow. [Contact the team](https://poolproof.com/get-in-touch) or call **(210) 245-7495** to get started.
</Note>
